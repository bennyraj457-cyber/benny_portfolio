## Brand & Style

Aureolin Botanica bridges classical botanical taxonomy, archival natural history, and modern phytochemical laboratory science. The design aesthetic is scholarly, precise, and editorial—blending antiquarian herbarium plates and Linnaean documentation with chromatographic telemetry and clinical bio-storage protocols.

The visual direction pairs an archival paper foundation with tactile, luminous aureolin yellow accents and deep forest botanic tones. The mood is academic, authentic, and scientific, balancing historic elegance with modern analytical rigor.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from vintage parchment paper, herbarium specimens, and laboratory reagents:

- **Neutral/Surface (`#fbf9f3`)**: Warm, deacidified paper ground creating a physical, catalog-like tactile feel.
- **Primary / Accent Yellow (`#f5d000` / `#6f5d00`)**: Derived from *Hypericum* stamen resin and yellow flavonoid pigment, used as high-visibility specimen flags, callouts, and key interaction triggers.
- **Secondary Herbarium Forest (`#4c6452` / `#cbe6d0`)**: Pressed foliage and vintage botanical ink tone used for secondary tags, botanical descriptors, and ecological indicators.
- **Inverse Surface (`#30312d`)**: Deep slate-charcoal used for spectral charts, technical data screens, and dark contrast plates.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy relies on a high-contrast juxtaposition:
1. **Editorial Serifs (Newsreader)**: Evokes archival folios, classical Latin binomial titles, and scholarly treatises. Italic forms are deployed extensively for taxonomic names and secondary subtitles.
2. **Technical Monospace (Space Mono)**: Injected for telemetry ribbons, accession tags, chemical formulas, and metadata indices, emphasizing the archival laboratory identity.

## Layout & Spacing

A 12-column layout structured around ample negative margins reminiscent of wide museum plate matting:
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 1-column stack, outer margin `1.25rem` (`margin`), section gap `space-lg`.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px)**: 2-column or 6/6 column split with `1.5rem` gutters (`gutter`).
- **Desktop (≥ 1024px)**: Standard 12-column grid with generous `3.5rem` lateral canvas margins (`margin-desktop`) and `2.5rem` gutters (`gutter-desktop`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through subtle tonal layering and soft, museum-grade matting shadows:
- **Tonal Stepping**: Surface tiers transition gently from `surface` (`#fbf9f3`) to `surface-container-low` (`#f5f3ee`), `surface-container` (`#f0eee8`), and `surface-container-high` (`#eae8e2`).
- **Archival Drop Shadows**: Specimen cards and callouts leverage warm, diffused ambient shadows (`shadow-sm`, `shadow-md`, `shadow-lg`) without harsh black boundaries.
- **Glassmorphism**: Translucent floating ribbons utilize `bg-surface/90 backdrop-blur-md` with hairline boundaries.
- **Specimen Contrast**: Inverted dark sheets (`inverse-surface`) provide rich contrast for scientific spectrometry visualizations and UV readings.

## Shapes

The design employs pill-shaped roundedness (`roundedness: 3`) for badges, navigation items, metrics, and call-to-action buttons (`rounded-full`), while larger containers and specimen plates preserve architectural softness with `rounded-lg` (1rem to 2rem) and `rounded-xl` (2rem to 3rem).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), filled with `primary-container` (`#f5d000`) and text set in uppercase `label-caps` (`#6a5900`). Hover scales brightness to `primary-fixed` (`#ffe167`) with an expanded accent halo (`hover:shadow-[0_0_0_2px_#FAF8F2,0_0_0_4px_#F5D000]`).
- **Secondary Action**: Pill-shaped in `surface-container` with subtle elevation and dark text.

### Tags & Chips
- Fully rounded pills (`rounded-full`) incorporating an optional pulsating status dot (`w-1.5 h-1.5 rounded-full bg-primary-container animate-pulse`).
- Typeset in uppercase `label-caps` with wide letter-spacing (`0.12em`).

### Cards & Specimen Plates
- Enclosed within `surface-container-low` or `surface-container-lowest` backgrounds.
- Feature delicate borders or shadow-sm lift, often hosting overlaid inspection callouts framed in blurred frosted cards.

### Telemetry Ribbons & Data Strips
- Horizontal, compact bars tracking continuous environmental variables (temperature, relative humidity, lux, CO₂).
- Rendered in `Space Mono` (`label-metric` / `code-catalog`) to maintain alignment across fluctuating numerical values.
